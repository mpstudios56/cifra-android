Cifra 2.1.4

  • The navigation bar no longer withdraws a piece at a time. Each button kept a clock of its own, so the one switched on last stayed out while the others had already tucked away: there is one clock for the whole column now, and they move together
  • The button that shows the order of the accounts has moved up into the navigation bar, above the eye, and leaves the bottom bar. It stays lit while the numbers are on show
  • Touching a template creates the movement at once. It enters the register with the date and time of that moment and opens for editing, usually of the amount. Before, it opened the card of a movement that did not yet exist
  • Quick entry is gone from the Templates and Scheduled tabs, where there was nothing to enter quickly
  • The planner leaves the menu. It is a button beside the filter, in the list of movements: what is still to come sits next to what has already happened, not three screens away
  • The buttons in the movements bar are in order: new, quick, filters, planner, search. And the magnifier is now the last icon in every bar of the app
  • In the planner the period has a button of its own, taken out of the filters: it is the question that screen exists to ask. The period chosen is now actually applied — the choice was read wrongly and thrown away every time — and at the top it appears by its own name rather than as two dates. The two dates appear only for “custom”: for “this month” they are worked out, not chosen. And only the periods that look ahead are offered: today, tomorrow and “this and last fiscal year” are gone
  • The note filter has been removed. A note is written in whatever words came to mind that day: it is not a key to search a year of movements by, and the search looks there anyway
  • The buttons at the foot of the filters match the other screens: the confirmation filled in green, the way out outlined
  • The date, the time and the state of a movement are three of a kind. They are no longer the grey shadowed buttons of fifteen years ago, and the state is no longer a square button between the other two
  • The calendar and the clock wear Cifra’s colours: the same rounded dark card as every other dialog, green in place of the system blue, numbers in cream. They were the last two pieces still dressed by Android
  • The list of states shows each one beside its own mark, in its own colour. They were five words in a row
  • In the ring of symbols the five states are drawn larger: they were coloured dots, because the request to enlarge them was ignored by that menu, which imposes its own measurements on symbols. And the earlier icons are back for “show in account”, “duplicate” and “change to transaction or transfer”
  • The two selection icons in Mass operations have been redrawn — they were pictures from fifteen years ago, blurred — and the Proceed button no longer breaks its word across two lines
  • The “rebuild” icon has been redrawn

Cifra 2.1.3

  • Folding a year no longer changes the balance. The total at the foot was worked out from what was left on screen; it is now worked out from every movement, because folding is a way of reading, not counting
  • A split keeps its payee, place and project. A split divides an amount between categories, not the shop one was in
  • The equals sign between the wheels and the calculator appears only inside a split, where it has something to do
  • The safe is a safe again, not a padlock
  • In the categories screen the bolt that reorders the tree has a mark of its own
  • The plus and minus that tell income from expense on a new category are in the app’s colours
  • Filtering on notes is gone from the movement filters: searching inside a note is what the magnifying glass is for
  • The button that closes the years shows their state

Cifra 2.1.2

  • The three buttons on the right start switched off and are turned on only by the screens that use them: they used to appear for an instant even where they have no part to play
  • Under the button that returns to today there were two arrows on top of each other, one left over from an earlier change. One remains
  • The buttons are a little fainter at rest, and the eye now matches them
  • Choosing a category has a screen of its own. It reused the row of a movement, filling two of its eight fields: it now shows the category’s symbol, its colour, its name, any note, and the mark that says it holds categories of its own
  • The four settings that remembered the last selection have been removed, and the behaviour they governed is now off
  • Two empty sections are gone from the settings

Cifra 2.1.1

  • The buttons on the right can be seen and hit. They were too faint and tucked almost entirely past the edge; they now stay visible, retire only a little, and offer a wider surface to touch
  • A fourth arrow, in the list of movements only: up to the newest movement, above the button that returns to today
  • The year dividers appear only where rows are movements with a date
  • The labels in the circular menus can be read in full

Cifra 2.1

The list of movements

  • Years fold and unfold. A dividing line appears before the first movement of each year: touch it and the year below folds away, and the arrow becomes a full stop. A folded year is no longer worked through, so closing one is instant even with ten thousand movements
  • Three buttons in a column on the right: the eye that hides amounts, an arrow down to the first movement ever recorded, and the one that comes back to today. They retire against the edge together and return on a touch, both in the main list and inside a single account
  • The sharing dot, the weekend colour and the time of day are applied as the list is built, so a change to the settings shows at once, without restarting the app
  • Inside an account, the last movement is no longer hidden behind the system buttons

Appearance

  • The account symbols have been redrawn from a professional set: body and drawing in two strengths of the same colour, so the shade chosen for the account stays visible in both. Every account has its own symbol, and four more are offered: deposit, briefcase, padlock, shield
  • Redrawn as well: the padlock on closed accounts, the filter marks, those of scheduled, planner and bulk operations, the drafts screen and the circular menu under the plus button
  • The drafts screen no longer uses Android’s standard parts: its own rows, green selection instead of the system teal, and a selection bar in the app’s colours
  • Account colours come from the same palette as the categories, and accounts restored from a backup can now decide whether the colour applies to the symbol, the row, or both

Fewer settings

  • Thirty-five switches have been removed. What they governed is now on by default: running balance, coloured fields, project, time of day, currency, payee, place, note, picture, buttons gathered in the menu under the plus, every status available
  • The remaining choices moved to where they are used. The order of payees, places and projects is set from a button in their own lists; that of templates from the filter button; week, fiscal year and rate provider are in Periods, currencies and rates
  • In the accounts screen the three dots have become the backup button, and in an account’s menu Delete is the last entry
  • One entry for the status and one for the copy: each opens a second level with the five statuses and the three ways of duplicating, instead of asking for a decision once and for all in the settings
  • In the summary, spending by category becomes income by category with a touch on the heading

Sharing, backup, widget

  • Sharing now has a direction. An account received can be opened and changed without being sent back to whoever shared it: that was the vicious circle which doubled balances. Sharing it onward is still possible, but it is a deliberate choice
  • Sharing the whole history now sends transfers coming in as well, which used to be left behind and made balances wrong
  • One widget with four buttons - movement, transfer, quick entry, templates - and Cifra’s own mark. The balance widgets are gone and nothing needs enabling
  • Home-screen shortcuts work again: they relied on a mechanism Android has not supported since version 8
  • A local backup no longer mentions Google Drive, and a backup that succeeds clears the notice of an earlier attempt that did not

Cifra 2.0

  • Drag a movement right or left to act on it, the way a mail app works. The two actions are chosen in the settings, one per direction: edit, duplicate, mark cleared, reconcile, delete - or nothing. Out of the box, right marks cleared and left deletes
  • Undo appears for a few seconds afterwards and puts everything back, deletion included: a swipe is a gesture one makes by accident, and without the way back it becomes something to be careful of rather than something to use
  • As the row slides, the colour of the action comes out from under it with its sign, on the side the finger came from: faint while the row would spring back, solid once it would not
  • The gesture waits until it is sure: nothing moves before the finger has gone further sideways than down, so taps and long presses reach the list exactly as they did

Cifra 1.9.1

  • Movements actually arrive. In 1.9 accounts, categories and places went across but not one movement: the receiving phone refused every row addressed to it, and movements written between two rounds went out with no name of their own
  • The signature is the pair’s code, not the phone. Somebody who changes phone stays themselves, and what arrived earlier has been signed again
  • Every movement that arrives says who wrote it: open it and the line “Written by” is there
  • The dot takes the colour you gave that person, on movements and on accounts that arrive, instead of always the same amber
  • The question is “Which movements to share?” — all of them, an opening balance, or only new ones — with Cancel and Save, asked on every save, in the account and in Shared accounts alike
  • The generated balance goes in as an opening line, worded the way the app words its own
  • Every screen sits inside the system bars. Some ran under the navigation keys, and an account’s first movement was invisible: it was there, it counted, it could not be seen
  • Deleting a movement updates the balance at once: the automatic exchange was opening a second connection to the database while the screen read the old total through the first

Tap and hold

  • A tap on an account opens its movements. Held down, the ring of symbols opens, now carrying every action once: the same entries no longer sit in two different menus
  • A tap on a movement opens its card, with the category's symbol and colour, the status, and the name of whoever wrote it in their colour
  • The sharing dot sits on the line of the account name instead of over the category symbol it used to cover
  • Menu entries answer the first tap
  • The bar carrying an account's name no longer has an empty band above it

Reading a movement

  • The state symbols have been redrawn. They were pictures made for a screen of fifteen years ago and were being blown up until they broke into squares: the same two signs, sharp at any size
  • A movement's card said its state twice; the one at the top stays, and its text takes the colour of the state
  • The monthly preview says what the spending was. It showed the note alone, and most movements have none: it now carries the payee, or the category, with whatever is left underneath

The exchange

  • It carries on outside the main screen. It started and stopped with that screen, so walking into an account stopped it until you came out again
  • The account list is read again on every visit, so a balance is never a round behind
  • A round every minute instead of every two, and the file in the shared folder is rewritten only when something has changed: it used to be written out identical every round, and the cloud synced it for nothing
  • A round that leaves something to review or to merge says so, with a notification from the phone: permission is asked the first time Sharing is opened, and can be granted later from Permissions if it was refused

Summary, budgets and reports

  • Spending by category turns into income by category with a tap on the card's heading, which is a finger tall; "show all" appears only when there are more than five categories
  • Under the period balance: "90% of income still on hand", and "No income in this period" when nothing came in
  • Each budget chooses whether shared movements count
  • Each report can be read with or without shared movements, from a button on the report itself

Cifra 1.9.0

  • Sharing works. Nothing used to arrive: the part that sends accounts, categories, payees and places was written and never called, and rows were named too late, so movements went out without saying which account they belonged to
  • You share with people, not with "the other phone". Add one with the +: a name, a colour and a code that goes on their phone too, against your name. That code is the pair. There can be more than one, and the accounts you share with Debora are not the ones you share with Antonio

  • The change log can be emptied, asked twice

  • A fault blocked importing and deleting old movements for anybody who had not picked a backup folder by hand
  • Importing next month's statement no longer brings the overlapping days in twice
  • The calculator is in the app's colours with one memory instead of four, and the amount wheels have been redrawn

For example, If I defined a currency for my VTI investments, set its trading currency as USD, and my home currency is TWD. With defined exchange rate for VTI->USD and USD->TWD, I can view my VTI's current balance value in TWD (tapping the balance at bottom right of account transaction list).

[+] Support currencies with decimal places > 2

Storage is still 64-bit signed integer with precision of 18~19 significant decimal digits, so currently limiting to max 12 decimal places. It's anticipated to be used with custom currencies to manage stocks/securities, not for managing cryptocurrencies with extreme precision (e.g. Ethereum need 256-bit or ~77 decimal digits).

Changing decimal places of a currency WILL NOT MODIFY EXISTING TRANSACTIONS. You need to manually update them. Remember backing up your data.
