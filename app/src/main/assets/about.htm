Cifra {version}

Personal finance manager for Android. Old-school and offline by design: no account, no cloud service, no telemetry. Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly enable Google Drive or Dropbox backup.

BE SURE TO BACKUP YOUR DATA REGULARLY.

Coming from another app?

Cifra reads what other money apps export: the CSV and QIF files nearly all of them can produce. It is under Menu → Import/Export. There is no need to start again from nothing — bring the history in and carry on.

Where this comes from

Cifra is a fork of Financisto Holo by Tiberius Teng, which is itself derived from Financisto by Denis Solonenko. Financisto was released in 2010 and last updated in 2019; Financisto Holo revived it, modernised the toolchain and keeps it maintained today.

Cifra builds on both. Everything that works here works because of their years of effort, and it is only possible because they released their work under a free licence.

Cifra is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Financisto, Financisto Holo, or their authors.

Known limitations

Google no longer grants SMS permissions to newly submitted apps, so automatic creation of transactions from bank SMS is not available. Import from statement files is the planned replacement.
