# Privacy Policy for Cifra

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Cifra is a personal finance manager for Android. It is free software, released
under the GNU General Public License v2.0, and its complete source code is public
at <https://github.com/mpstudios56/cifra-android>. Anything stated below can be
checked against that source.

## The short version

Cifra has no servers. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and shows no adverts.
Everything you enter stays in a database on your own phone, unless you explicitly
set up a backup to a cloud account that belongs to you.

## What stays on your device

Your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, payees, projects, attached
pictures and any notes are stored in a database inside the app's private storage
on your phone. Cifra does not transmit any of it.

If you use the unlock code or the fingerprint lock, those settings are kept on the
device as well. The unlock code never leaves the phone and is never included in any
export.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask

**Backups you start yourself.** You can save a backup to a folder you choose on the
phone, or send it to Google Drive or Dropbox. Nothing is uploaded unless you set up
that connection and either run a backup or switch on the automatic one. The data
goes to your own account with those providers, not to us. Their handling of it is
governed by their own privacy policies.

When Google Drive is used, Cifra asks only for permission to see and manage the
files it creates itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.

**Settings export.** You can export your app settings to a readable file. It
deliberately excludes the unlock code, the fingerprint options and the Dropbox and
Google Drive credentials.

**Sharing a backup.** If you choose to send a backup somewhere else, for instance by
email, the destination is entirely your choice.

## Keeping one set of books with another person

Cifra+ can keep two phones in step, so that two people writing in the same
accounts each see what the other has written. It works through a folder in a
cloud account that already belongs to one of you - Google Drive, Dropbox, or any
folder your phone can reach - and nowhere else.

There is no server of ours in the middle. Nobody signs in to anything belonging
to the maker of this app, no account is created, and no copy of your movements
is ever sent to us: we could not read them if we wanted to, because they never
pass through anything we run. What travels is one file, written into your own
folder and read back by the other phone.

The two phones recognise each other by a code the two of you choose and type in
yourselves. Only the accounts you tick are shared; the rest stay on your phone
alone. Switching the feature off stops it, and the file in the folder is yours
to delete whenever you like.

## Files you open in Cifra

You can hand Cifra a file exported by another finance app and have it read the
transactions into your own accounts. The file is read on the phone and nothing
about it is sent anywhere. Cifra sees only the file you pick, and only while it
is importing it: it is not given access to the rest of your storage.

Before writing anything from such a file, Cifra takes a full backup of what you
already have, into the backup folder you chose. That backup stays on the phone
unless you have connected Google Drive or Dropbox yourself.

## Optional features that read sensitive information

If you switch it on, Cifra can read incoming notifications in order to recognise
payment alerts from your bank or wallet app and offer to record them as
transactions. This reading happens entirely on the phone, the content is not sent
anywhere, and the feature is off until you grant the permission yourself. You can
withdraw the permission at any time in the Android settings.

Locations attached to transactions are values you type or import. Cifra does not
read your phone's GPS or location services.

## What Cifra does not do

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any
kind. No third-party library in the app performs any of these. Cifra does not
create an account for you and does not ask you to sign in, except for the optional
Google Drive or Dropbox connection described above.

## Children

Cifra is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the database and all settings from the phone. Backups
you previously placed in a folder, on Google Drive or on Dropbox are yours to
delete, as they live in your storage and your accounts.
